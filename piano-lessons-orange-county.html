What In-Home Piano Lessons Look Like

Piano is where so many musicians begin, and for good reason: it makes music visible. Every note has a place, and a beginner can be playing a melody the whole family recognizes sooner than they expect. Music and Mastery brings private, one-on-one piano lessons to families across Orange County, from Irvine and Tustin to Newport Beach, Costa Mesa and Lake Forest. Instead of loading up after a full school day, your instructor comes to your home and teaches at your own piano or keyboard, in a setting where your child already feels comfortable. Every teacher is DOJ and FBI background-checked through LiveScan. Whether your child is five and learning where middle C lives, a teen working toward the songs they love, or an adult returning to an instrument from years ago, every lesson is built around your goals and pace. Start with a free first lesson, month-to-month with a money-back guarantee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need a piano or keyboard before the first lesson?

No. A full-size keyboard works perfectly to start, so you don't need a grand piano. Your instructor will recommend the right option for your space and budget at the first lesson. Book your free first lesson to get started.

How young can my child start piano lessons?

We teach piano to students as young as five, all the way through adult beginners. Lessons are fully one-on-one, so the pace and songs are matched to your child's age and goals.

Which Orange County cities do you cover for in-home piano lessons?

Our instructors come to your home across Orange County, including Irvine, Newport Beach, Costa Mesa, Lake Forest, and Tustin. Every session is private and taught in your home. Your first piano lesson is free so you can try it risk-free.
